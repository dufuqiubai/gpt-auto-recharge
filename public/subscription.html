How to download an invoice
1

Sign in to ChatGPT

Sign in at chatgpt.com with the Plus or Pro account.

2

Open subscription and billing

Click your avatar at the bottom left, then Settings → Account → Manage.

3

Open payment history

On the Stripe billing page, find the charge and click View invoice or Download invoice to save the PDF.

4

Update billing details (optional)

To add a company name or tax ID, update Billing details. Later invoices use the new details.

FAQ
  • Lookup failed? Confirm the session is from a signed-in ChatGPT account and the access token has not expired.
  • Which currency is the invoice in? It matches the currency Stripe actually charged. Some accounts do not return a currency field.
  • How soon can I download it? The charge usually appears on the billing page within a few minutes after payment.
  • Can’t find the invoice? Make sure you are signed in to the paid account, and check whether the subscription is on a team or business workspace or through the App Store.
